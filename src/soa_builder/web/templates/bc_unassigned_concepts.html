About This Report

This report lists CDISC Library biomedical concepts that exist in the full concept catalogue but are not classified under any biomedical concept category. These concepts may represent recently added or uncategorized clinical observations. Study builders can use this report to identify gaps in category coverage and decide which concepts to assign directly to study activities.
